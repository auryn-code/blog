This is my first blog post.

I want this place to become a quiet corner for recording ideas, research notes, technical experiments, and fragments of everyday life. A personal website should feel less like a polished announcement board and more like a long-running conversation with yourself: sometimes precise, sometimes unfinished, but always honest.

As a Ph.D. student, I spend a lot of time thinking about learning systems, control, safety, and how complex things behave when they are placed in the real world. As a programmer, I also enjoy the craft of building small tools, polishing interfaces, and making abstract ideas visible through code.

So this blog starts here.

Not with a grand declaration, just with a first note:

I will write, build, revise, and keep going.
